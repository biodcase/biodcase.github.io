    BioDCASE Workshop

Details

This year's BioDCASE Workshop will be held online on the 23rd of October.

The program will include invited speakers on the topic of bioacoustics as well as poster sessions of technical reports. The BioDCASE workshop will be hosted the week before the DCASE workshop on the 28-29th of October in Boston.

Participants can also submit a scientific paper to DCASE. In this case, the paper must respect the structure of a scientific publication and be prepared according to the provided DCASE paper instructions and template. You can find more details here.

The workshop is free but will require registration to attend. Please register here.
