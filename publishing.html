Introduction

The DCASE research community has grown rapidly in recent years, and the number of academic publications from the community has increased steadily over the last decade. This page collects information about academic publications related to DCASE research topics.

Topics

Research in the DCASE can be divided into multiple distinct research areas. The publishing statistics focus on seven major research areas:

  • Acoustic scene classification (ASC)
  • Sound event detection (SED)
  • Audio tagging
  • Audio captioning
  • Sound event localization and detection (SELD)
  • Anomalous sound detection
  • Bioacoustic event detection
